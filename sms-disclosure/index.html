Program Description

RankLocal sends SMS text messages to users who opt in through our website forms or by providing a phone number during the partner application or campaign setup process. Messages may include: responses to your inquiry, application status updates, campaign setup and onboarding steps, account and billing notifications, call-delivery alerts, and customer care messages.

Opt-In

You opt in to receive SMS from RankLocal by submitting your phone number through a form on ranklocall.com and checking the SMS consent box, or by texting us first. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with RankLocal (for example, during campaign onboarding you may receive several messages; afterwards, messages are occasional).

Fees

Message and data rates may apply. Charges are billed by your wireless carrier according to your mobile plan.

Opt-Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you send STOP, we will send a final message confirming you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further messages. To rejoin, sign up again through our forms or reply START.

Help

If you experience issues or want more information, reply HELP to any message, email admin@ranklocall.com, or call (833) 779-9997.
