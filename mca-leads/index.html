What is an MCA lead?

A merchant cash advance lead is a business owner actively seeking revenue-based funding. RankLocal delivers them as exclusive inbound phone calls or live transfers - not as recycled data lists.

Real-time vs aged MCA leads - which should I buy?

Real-time exclusive calls convert at multiples of aged lists because the merchant initiates contact at peak intent. Aged leads are cheaper per record but cost more per funded deal once connect and contact rates are factored in.

Do you sell MCA leads with bank statements?
