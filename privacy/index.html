Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

RankLocal ("RankLocal," "we," "us," or "our") operates ranklocall.com and provides local SEO and pay-per-call lead generation services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: name, company name, email address, phone number, business address, service details, and campaign preferences submitted through our forms (including the partner application and campaign setup forms).
  • Call data: when calls are routed through our tracking numbers, we collect caller ID, call time, duration, and call recordings where permitted by law.
  • Automatically collected data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referral source, collected via cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve our lead generation and SEO services
  • To route, track, record, and bill qualified calls
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • To send service communications, including SMS where you have opted in
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use

3. SMS Communications and Consent

RANK LOCAL LLC may send SMS text messages to customers and prospective customers who have explicitly opted in to receive such communications through our website forms.

Types of SMS Messages

Depending on the consent provided, messages may include:

  • Marketing and promotional messages, including special offers, promotions, and information about our services.
  • Informational and transactional messages, including responses to inquiries, appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, service updates, follow-ups, and customer support communications.

SMS Consent

SMS consent is collected through separate, optional checkboxes for marketing/promotional communications and informational/transactional communications. Both checkboxes are unchecked by default.

Providing consent to receive SMS messages is voluntary. Consent to receive marketing or promotional SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing products or services.

Message Frequency and Charges

Message frequency varies depending on customer interactions, communication preferences, and the services requested.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Support

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to any SMS message.

For assistance, reply HELP or contact RANK LOCAL LLC using the contact information provided on our website.

Mobile Information and SMS Consent Data

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers or subcontractors when necessary to support business operations, such as customer service or messaging services. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Security

RANK LOCAL LLC uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information collected through our website and SMS communications. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Additional SMS Information

For additional information about our SMS messaging program, including consent requirements, message frequency, message and data rates, opt-out instructions, and customer support, please review our SMS Disclosure.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with: (a) service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, form processing, call tracking, analytics) under confidentiality obligations; (b) buyer partners, solely to route and fulfill the service you requested when you call a tracking number; and (c) authorities when required by law.

5. Call Recording

Calls placed through our tracking numbers may be recorded for quality assurance, billing verification, and dispute resolution. Where required by applicable law, callers are notified of recording at the start of the call.

6. Data Retention & Security

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at admin@ranklocall.com.

8. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be posted on this page.
