Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how RankLocal ("we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on ranklocall.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember preferences, and measure how visitors use it. Similar technologies include pixels, local storage, and session identifiers.

2. Cookies We Use

CategoryPurposeExamples
Strictly necessaryCore site functions: page delivery, security, form submission (e.g., embedded application forms).Hosting/CDN cookies, Tally form session
AnalyticsUnderstand site usage — pages visited, traffic sources, time on page — so we can improve content.Google Analytics or similar
FunctionalRemember choices such as scheduling-widget preferences.Calendly embed
AttributionMeasure which pages and keywords generate calls so buyers are billed accurately.Call-tracking parameters

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information, and no SMS opt-in data is collected via cookies or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some embedded tools set their own cookies, governed by their own policies: form provider (Tally), scheduling (Calendly), and analytics providers. We do not control these cookies.

4. How to Control Cookies

  • Browser settings: all major browsers let you block or delete cookies (look under Settings → Privacy). Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the site, such as application forms.
  • Analytics opt-out: you can install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Do Not Track / GPC: where legally required, we honor applicable browser opt-out signals.

5. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as our tools change. The date above reflects the latest revision.
