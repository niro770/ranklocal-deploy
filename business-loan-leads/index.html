Frequently Asked Questions

How much do business loan leads cost?

Pricing is per qualified call and varies by coverage area and volume. Tell us your coverage area, daily cap and hours, and we quote the price per call and expected volume before you commit. You start with a prepay of 10 billable calls.

Are your business loan leads exclusive?

Yes. Every call and form lead is delivered to exactly one buyer. We never resell or share leads between clients.

Where do the leads come from?

Google and Bing search campaigns targeting merchants actively looking for funding. No social media traffic, no incentivized clicks, no aged data dressed up as real-time.

What counts as a billable call?

An inbound call from your coverage area lasting over 60 seconds. Shorter calls, duplicates and wrong numbers are not billed, and disputes are reviewed within 48 hours.
